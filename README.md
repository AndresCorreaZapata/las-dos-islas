Las Dos Islas™ — EFA Country Simulator™

Las Dos Islas™ — EFA Country Simulator™ is an interactive economic strategy simulator created by Andrés Correa Zapata, based on EFA — Estrategias de Flujo Adaptativo.

The game puts the player in the role of an economic decision-maker. The objective is not simply to make the economy grow, but to protect its critical flow, adapt to disruptions and improve economic performance over time.

What is EFA?

EFA — Estrategias de Flujo Adaptativo is a strategic framework developed by Andrés Correa Zapata for decision-making in environments characterized by volatility, disruption and uncertainty.

In simple terms, EFA asks the player to:

Protect what matters.

Invest for the future.

Grow the economy without breaking its flow.

Adapt when conditions change.

Learn from the consequences of decisions.

You do not need to know EFA or read the book before playing. The simulator is designed so that the player can discover the principles through the experience of making decisions.

📊 EFA Score™

The simulator includes an EFA SCORE™ designed to evaluate the quality of the player's strategic decisions across multiple dimensions rather than rewarding growth alone.

The score is part of the pedagogical design of the simulator: the player can see that apparently attractive decisions may create hidden weaknesses in wages, productivity, flow, capacity, resilience or other economic dimensions.

🌍 Economic disruptions

The simulator introduces events in intuitive real-world terms, including disruptions related to:

logistics and international trade;

energy availability and costs;

capital requirements after major shocks;

external demand;

health and productive capacity.

The intention is to make economic reasoning accessible without eliminating strategic complexity.

🎓 Educational purpose

Las Dos Islas™ is designed to be useful for:

students of economics, business and related disciplines;

university professors;

executives and managers;

strategy and supply-chain professionals;

people interested in economics who have no previous knowledge of EFA.

The game can be played independently and can also serve as a starting point for classroom discussion about economic trade-offs, resilience, productivity, investment and adaptation.

🧪 Stable baseline

Simulator engine: v0.6.3 — Level 3 Public Stable

Version v0.6.3 is the validated stable baseline of the simulator engine.

It was tested through complete playthroughs from Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 and is being preserved as the reference baseline for future development.

Future levels will be developed as new, versioned work rather than by altering the fundamental parameters of the validated v0.6.3 engine.

🚀 Roadmap

The project is intended to evolve progressively:

Levels 1–3: public and playable.

Level 4: next development stage.

Level 5 and subsequent levels: progressively deeper strategic and economic challenges.

Future versions may expand the educational, university and international reach of the simulator.

Future levels may introduce greater interdependence, uncertainty and strategic pressure while preserving economic coherence.

📚 EFA

Las Dos Islas™ is part of the broader EFA intellectual framework developed by Andrés Correa Zapata.

EFA integrates ideas related to adaptive flow, constraints, demand-driven thinking, resilience, productivity, investment and strategic adaptation.

The simulator is intended to make these ideas experiential: the player learns by deciding and observing the consequences.
